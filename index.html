MEET INNOVATION BAND

Our story.
Our sound.

Innovation Band is a modern and cover band initiated by MSU-MCEST Campus Superintendent Atty. Jamaloden A. Basar. The band began in late 2023 with five members, coordinated by Engr. Rodel Apao and led by keyboardist Rimar Mongcupa.

Today, Innovation Band is an academic organization that performs at campus events, for visiting guests, and at permitted outside events. Its repertoire spans old pop, rock, acoustic, reggae, OPM, and other styles suited to its academic purpose.

GOOD TO KNOW

A few quick
answers.

Wondering how a booking works? Start here, and send us a note if you have another question.

Ask us directly ↗
What kinds of events can we play?+

We accept requests for campus events, birthdays, weddings, fiesta concerts, business openings, and appearances as a minor performer at larger concerts, subject to approval and availability.

How far ahead should I book?+

Please send your request at least 2–3 weeks ahead of the event. The coordinator will confirm availability.

What music can I request?+

The band plays old pop, rock, acoustic, reggae, OPM, and other suitable genres. Offensive or hate-driven songs and excessively loud material are not accepted. The band confirms final song choices.

Where does the band perform?+

The service area is within Lanao del Norte. Include the event location in your request so the band can review it.

What does the venue need to provide?+

The event site must have an available electricity source. Include any details about the performance space and equipment in your request.

How long is a typical performance?+

A typical booking is 3–5 sets, with about 8 songs per set. Tell us your event schedule and we can discuss the format.
