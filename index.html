GOOD TO KNOW

A few quick
answers.

Wondering how a booking works? Start here, and send us a note if you have another question.

Ask us directly ↗
What kinds of events can we book the band for?+

We welcome requests for university programs, community gatherings, celebrations, and other special occasions. Share a little about your event and we’ll let you know what fits.

How far ahead should I send a request?+

Earlier is better, especially for larger events. Send your preferred date and details, and the band coordinator can check availability.

Can I request specific songs?+

Absolutely. Browse the sample song list and include your favorites or special requests in the booking form. We’ll confirm which arrangements are available.

What does the band need at the venue?+

Needs depend on the lineup and space. Include venue details, performance area, sound equipment, and any access notes in your request so we can discuss the setup.

How does the booking request work?+

Fill in the form below and choose “Prepare email request.” Your email app will open with the event details ready to send to the band contact.
